Peeno's Great Adventure

Peeno's Great Adventure was a project I worked on as part of my Level 3 year 1 game development course in the end of 2024 and start of 2025. The task was to build a proof of concept for a new 3D platformer titled: Peeno's Great Adventure.

I decided to theme my project around the idea of time and seasons passing. To achieve this, I split the level into 4 sections. Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. Each season would have its own colour palette and scenery would change over the course of the year to show the passage of time. I decided to have one minigame per season. This would give the player enough time to experience each season and would set a good pace for the game and the different minigames would make it a fun and engaging game.

Chainmail Info

Some useful info I've put together on chainmail

Where to buy jump rings:

The Queen Ring

https://thequeenring.com/

I find this source is cheaper for bulk orders, for stainless steel rings and is really good quality, but can be kinda rough on stock of different ring sizes

Wraithmaille:

https://www.wraithmaille.co.uk

A bit more expensive but a better range of ring sizes, better for anodized aluminium rings (the colourful ones) You can get some charms to go on jewellery from wraithmaille if you'd like :P I'd also recommend getting a load of lobster clasps. I normally get them from wraithmaille and get size of 12mm

What size rings to get:

You'll want a load of normal sized jump rings for weaves, and a load of smaller ones attaching clasps and for smaller bits. I also like weaving with smaller jump rings sometimes, but it can be more fiddly especially while learning new weaves.

Sizes still confuse me a little, but...

for my normal rings I've got 1.5mm x 7mm (1.5 is the thickness of the wire, 7 is the rings inner diameter)

For my small ones I got 4.11mm inner diameter

Ring materials:

Steel:

Steel is better in most ways apart from you can't get different colours. Its heavier, and stronger.

Aluminium::

Aluminium is fun in that it can be anodized into pretty colours :333

M.A.I.Lhttps://www.mailleartisans.org/weaves/

This is a really good place to learn new weaves. Especially any in the beginner section come with really good diagrams.
